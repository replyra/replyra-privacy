Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about setup, Groq BYOK, Pro, data, and everyday use.

AI output requires human review. Always verify claims and customer details before sending or publishing.
What is Replyra?

Replyra is a Chrome side-panel writing assistant for Amazon sellers. It can help draft customer responses, rewrite listing text, and review wording that may need a closer look.

Does Replyra send replies to Amazon automatically?

No. Replyra creates text that you review, edit, and copy yourself. It does not automatically send customer messages or publish listings.

Do I need an API key?

Yes. The standard Replyra setup requires a Groq API key that you control. In this version, the extension sends your generation request directly to Groq using your key.

Is Groq included with Replyra Pro?

No. The Replyra subscription and Groq API access are separate. Groq sets its own free-tier allowances, prices, and model availability.

What is the free plan limit?

Replyra currently provides up to 10 generations per day on the Free plan. The limit is enforced inside the extension. API-provider limits also apply.

What does Pro add?

Pro removes Replyra’s local Free-plan daily generation limit and enables Batch Listing Rewrite of multiple bullets (up to five per batch). Groq rate and usage limits still apply.

Can I rewrite a title, bullet point, or description?

Yes. The Listing tool supports those three modes. Always verify product specifications, limits, and claims before using the result.

What does Policy Guard do?

Policy Guard and claim checks flag selected wording that may be risky or unsupported. They may miss problems or show unnecessary warnings, and do not guarantee factual accuracy or compliance with marketplace rules.

Can I save and delete drafts?

Yes. Saving is on by default for new installations (up to 50 locally saved drafts). You can turn it off, search saved text, delete selected items, or clear the library in the extension.

Where is my data stored?

The Groq key, settings, selected profiles, saved drafts, and local usage information are stored in Chrome extension storage on your device. Text you choose to generate is sent to Groq. License verification sends membership details and a pseudonymous device identifier through Cloudflare to Whop.

How many devices can use my Pro license?

The current licensing system binds an eligible membership to one extension/device identifier. If you change devices or erase this identifier, contact Replyra support for assistance.

What if I cannot activate my license?

First check your membership status and Internet connection. If the issue persists, contact support with a brief description and any non-sensitive error message. Do not email your Groq API key.

What is the refund policy?

Your applicable refund, cancellation, and renewal terms are shown in your Whop checkout and membership details. Check those terms before subscribing, or contact support for clarification.

Is Replyra affiliated with Amazon?

No. Replyra is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.

How can I contact support?

Email replyra.support@gmail.com. Never send your API key, license key, or private customer information in a support message.

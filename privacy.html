Replyra Privacy Policy

Version 2.0.0

Replyra helps Amazon sellers write customer replies and listing bullets, and flags risky phrases before they send. This page explains what data Replyra handles.

Data stays on your device

Your settings, daily usage count, override log, cached model list, and Groq API key are stored locally on your device in chrome.storage.local. Replyra has no server of its own and cannot see or access this data.

Where requests go

  • Generation requests (the customer message, your context, or your bullet text, plus formatting instructions) are sent directly from your browser to api.groq.com only when you click Generate, using your own Groq API key.
  • Your Groq API key is stored on your device only and is sent only to api.groq.com.

Groq processes requests under its own privacy policy.

No analytics or telemetry

Replyra does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry, does not collect personal data, and does not load remote code.

Policy Guard

Policy Guard runs entirely on your device. Its text checks are not sent anywhere. If you override a warning, the time and rule names are saved locally on your device in the override log.

Deleting your data

Open Replyra Options and choose Delete all data, or remove the extension from Chrome. Either way, everything Replyra stored on your device is erased.

Not affiliated with Amazon

Replyra is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon. You are responsible for every message you send.

Replyra Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0

Replyra helps Amazon sellers write replies and flags risky phrases before they send. This page explains what data Replyra handles.

Data stays on your device

Your settings, daily usage count, override log, cached model list, and keys are stored on your device in chrome.storage.local. Replyra has no server of its own and cannot see this data.

Where requests go

  • Generation requests (the customer message, your context, or your bullet text, plus instructions) are sent only to api.groq.com, using your own Groq key.
  • Your Groq key is stored on your device only and is sent only to api.groq.com.
  • When you activate a Pro license, your license key and an instance name ("Replyra") are sent to api.lemonsqueezy.com to activate and validate it, and to deactivate it if you ask. The license key is also kept on your device so it can be re-checked.

Groq and Lemon Squeezy process data under their own privacy policies.

No analytics or telemetry

Replyra does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry, and does not load remote code.

Policy Guard

Policy Guard runs entirely on your device. Its text checks are not sent anywhere. If you override a warning, the time and rule names are saved locally in the override log.

Deleting your data

Open Replyra Options and choose Delete all data, or remove the extension from Chrome. Either way, everything Replyra stored on your device is erased. To free a Pro activation, choose Deactivate in Options first.

Not affiliated with Amazon

Replyra is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon. You are responsible for every message you send.

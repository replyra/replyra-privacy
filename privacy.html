Privacy Policy

How Replyra stores information on your device and processes requests through Groq, Cloudflare, and Whop.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. About Replyra

Replyra is a browser extension for writing customer messages and rewriting product text. You decide what text to submit, review generated content, and choose whether to copy it. Replyra does not automatically publish listings or send customer messages.

2. Information stored locally

Replyra uses Chrome extension storage for preferences, selected model, brand voice and profile settings, local usage counters, saved text, API key, license state, and a pseudonymous extension/device identifier. For new installations, local saving of generated text is enabled by default, for up to 50 items, until you turn it off or clear saved items. Data in your saved text may include whatever information you entered.

3. AI requests and BYOK

In this release, generation requests are sent directly from your extension to Groq over HTTPS using your own Groq API key. The material sent can include customer messages, product descriptions, context, selected tone, and brand instructions. Groq separately processes data under its published terms and privacy practices. Do not include customer information that is not needed for the writing task.

4. License validation

To activate or check Pro, Replyra sends your membership/license key and a pseudonymous extension/device identifier to Replyra’s licensing service hosted on Cloudflare. The service may contact Whop to verify subscription eligibility. Cloudflare D1 may retain a membership-to-identifier association for license binding. This identifier is not a verified physical hardware identifier.

5. What Replyra uses data for

These data are used to perform requested writing tasks, remember selected settings and saved items, provide subscription access, and maintain basic service operations. Replyra does not include advertising or sell submitted text to data brokers. Operational metadata may be processed by the infrastructure and AI providers according to their policies and configured logging.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

You can disable saved-text storage, remove individual saved items, clear the library, export non-secret locally stored data, or clear local Replyra data from Options. Ordinary local clearing preserves the pseudonymous device identifier so an existing Pro license can be reactivated; an explicitly requested full device erase also deletes that identifier and may require support assistance to restore Pro access. Deleting local data does not automatically delete records already processed by Groq, Cloudflare, or Whop. Third-party data retention depends on those services; Replyra does not promise a universal deletion period.

7. Security

Network requests to the disclosed API endpoints use HTTPS. Your Groq key remains in browser extension storage in this standard BYOK mode; it is not included in Replyra data exports. Browser and device security remain important, and no local application can guarantee protection against a compromised device.

8. AI output and third-party marketplaces

Generated output and automated claim checks can be wrong or incomplete. They are not a guarantee of compliance with Amazon or other marketplace requirements. You are responsible for reviewing every customer reply and product claim before use. Replyra is not affiliated with Amazon.

9. Service providers

Groq provides AI inference when you generate; Whop handles subscription status; Cloudflare hosts Replyra licensing infrastructure and D1 records. Their privacy notices govern their independent processing and retention. The optional experimental generation gateway is not enabled in this published direct-BYOK release.

10. Policy changes

If Replyra enables new data-processing paths (for example, routing generation requests through a Replyra-hosted gateway), this policy and in-product disclosures must be updated before users are moved to that mode. Material changes will be communicated in the product or on this page as appropriate.

11. Limited use of data

Replyra’s use of information received from Chrome APIs is intended to follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for product functionality and related operations described here.

12. Contact

For privacy questions and requests involving license-binding records, email replyra.support@gmail.com. Do not send API keys, license keys, or customer personal information by email.
